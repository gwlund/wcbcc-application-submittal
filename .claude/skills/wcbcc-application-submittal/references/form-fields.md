# County Application Form Fields

The Whatcom County "Advisory Group Application" is a two-step CivicPlus form
at
https://www.whatcomcounty.us/FormCenter/Boards-and-Commissions-16/Board-and-Commission-Application-88

**Source and date.**  Step One was read from the live form on 2026-11-26.
Step Two was transcribed from the CivicPlus confirmation email of a
submission made on 2026-11-26, which echoes each question's exact text.  If
the live form differs from this file, the live form wins.

## Form Behavior

- The form opens with a public-records statement: everything submitted is
  available to the County Council, the County Executive and the public.
- Signing in to a County account is optional.  Without signing in, progress
  is not saved, so a candidate who closes the tab starts over.  This is the
  main reason to prepare every answer before opening the form.
- Step Two only appears after Step One is complete and Continue is clicked.
- Long answers keep their paragraph breaks.
- The form does not show a character limit on the long answers.  Answers of
  about 1,100 to 1,300 characters were accepted on 2026-11-26.
- After submission, CivicPlus emails a copy titled "Online Form Submittal:
  Advisory Group Application" to the address entered in Step One.

## Step One: Contact Details

The candidate types these.  Do not collect them into the answers file unless
the candidate asks, since the file exists to hold drafted answers, not
personal records.

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Title (Dr., Miss, Mr., Mrs., Ms., Mx.) | No |
| First Name | Yes |
| Last Name | Yes |
| Today's Date | Yes |
| Street Address | Yes |
| City | Yes |
| Zip | Yes |
| Do you live in Whatcom County? (Yes / No) | Yes |
| Do you have a different mailing address? | No |
| Primary Telephone | Yes |
| Secondary Telephone | No |
| Email Address | Yes |

## Step Two: Questions

Question text is verbatim from the confirmation email.

1. **Name of Advisory Group** (a picklist): choose "Business and Commerce
   Advisory Committee".
   - **Business and Commerce Advisory Committee Positions** (appears after
     the committee is chosen): the sector seat, for example "Manufacturing".
2. **Do you meet the residency, employment, and/or affiliation requirements
   of the position for which you're applying?**  Yes / No.
3. **Which Council district do you live in?**  District number.
4. **Have you ever been a member of this Advisory Group**  Yes / No.
   - **If yes, please list dates:** free text, for example "Current member,
     partial term 2026 to January 31, 2027".
5. **Do you or your spouse have a financial interest in or are you an
   employee or officer of any business or agency that does business with
   Whatcom County?**  Yes / No.
   - **If yes, please explain:** one line is enough.
6. **Have you declared candidacy (as defined by RCW 42.17A.055) for a paid
   elected office in any jurisdiction within the county?**  Yes / No.
- **You may attach a resume or detailed summary of experience,
  qualifications, & interest in response to the following questions:**
  file upload, optional.  A PDF is the safe format.
7. **Please describe your occupation (or former occupation if retired),
   qualifications, professional and/or community activities, and
   education:**  long text.
8. **Please describe why you're interested in serving on this Advisory
   Group.**  long text.
- **References (please include daytime telephone number):**  free text.
- **Appointment Requirements:** a checkbox, "I understand and agree".
  Ask the candidate to read the requirement text on the form before
  checking it.
- **Signature of applicant:** the candidate types their full name.
- **Place Signed / Submitted:** city and state, for example "Bellingham, WA".
