# Committee Facts

Facts about the Whatcom County Business and Commerce Advisory Committee
(WCBCC), each with its source.  Seats and deadlines change every cycle; check
the dates below before relying on them, and update this file when a newer
roster comes out.

   2026-10-28, read 2026-11-26.
3. County Council office reappointment notice for the 2027 cycle (deadline
   below), received by a current member in 2026.

## Committee Basics

From source 1:

- **Purpose:** advises the Whatcom County Council on regulations and
  policies that could impact local businesses, industry, or economic
  development.
- **Enabling statute:** Whatcom County Code (WCC) 2.128; Ordinance 2018-030
  and Ordinance 2019-031.
- **Membership:** 23 members.  No two appointed members may represent or be
  employed by the same company or organization.
- **Term:** four years.  A partial term fills the remainder of a vacated
  seat.
- **Meetings:** generally monthly, 11:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., date and
  location vary.
- **Staff:** the Port of Bellingham supports the committee.  Tyler Schroeder,
  Director of Economic Development (tylers@portofbellingham.com), and Kori
  Olsen, Economic Development Project Manager (360-715-5118,
  KoriO@portofbellingham.com).

## Current Cycle

- **Deadline (2027 cycle):** Tuesday, January 5, 2027, 10:00 a.m., to the
  County Council Office (source 3).  Confirm with the Council office at
  360-778-5010; this committee did not appear on the Current Vacancies page
  as of its 2026-10-28 update (source 2).
- **Appointing body:** the County Council appoints the voting sector seats
  and the education seats (source 1).
- **Mail option:** applications can also be mailed to the County Council
  Office, Whatcom County Courthouse, 311 Grand Avenue, Suite 105,
  Bellingham, WA 98225 (source 2).

## Non-Voting Seats

From source 1:

- **Education (Council-appointed):** two seats for higher education (Western
  Washington University, Whatcom Community College, Northwest Indian College,
  Bellingham Technical College) and one for public K-12.
- **Permanent members (designees allowed):** County Council, County
  Executive, Bellingham Mayor, Port of Bellingham Executive Director, and the
  Small Cities Caucus representative.

A candidate related to a permanent member should disclose it under
Question 5.
