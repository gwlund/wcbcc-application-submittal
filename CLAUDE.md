# WCBCC Application Submittal

This repository exists to help a candidate apply for a seat on the Whatcom
County Business and Commerce Advisory Committee.  When the person in the
session mentions applying, a seat, or the County form, use the
`wcbcc-application-submittal` skill in `.claude/skills/`.

## Privacy Rule

This repository is public.  Candidate drafts, addresses, phone numbers and
references belong under `applications/`, which is git-ignored.  Never stage
or commit anything under `applications/`, and never move candidate content
into any other folder.

## Maintenance

Committee facts live in
`.claude/skills/wcbcc-application-submittal/references/committee-facts.md`.
Every fact there carries a source and date; keep that pattern when updating
it.
